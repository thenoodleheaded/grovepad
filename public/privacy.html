Privacy

Grovepad is built local-first. The short version: if you do not sign in, nothing you make ever leaves your device. If you do sign in, we store the boards you asked us to sync and almost nothing else.

Last updated: 12 August 2026

The short version

  • Your boards are stored on your own device first. That is the real copy.
  • There is no advertising and no advertising tracker anywhere in Grovepad. We do not build a profile of you.
  • We count one thing — that the app was opened — and you can switch even that off in Settings. It is described in full below.
  • We never sell or rent your data, and we never train anything on your boards.
  • You can export everything, at any time, for free, in open formats.
  • You can delete your account and your cloud data whenever you want.

Using Grovepad without an account

Guest mode is a real mode, not a trial. Your boards live in your browser's own storage on that device. We do not receive them, cannot read them, and do not know they exist. If you clear your browser storage or uninstall the app, they are gone — so export a copy if they matter to you.

In guest mode the only network requests Grovepad makes are for the app files themselves, the anonymous open count described below, and any optional integration you deliberately turn on (also below).

What we store when you sign in

Signing in creates an account so your boards can sync between your devices. We store:

  • Your account. Your email address and the sign-in identity your provider gives us, plus a display name and a profile colour if you set them.
  • Your synced boards. The contents of the canvases you sync — widgets, text, numbers, layout — stored as compressed documents.
  • Attachments. Pictures, audio, video and PDFs you drop onto a board.
  • Version history. Earlier snapshots of your boards, so you can go back.
  • Shared-canvas records. For a canvas you share, who is invited and what they are allowed to do, plus the live editing state and any comments.
  • Subscription status. Whether your plan is trialling, active, or lapsed, when the current period ends, and the customer identifier our payment provider gives us.

Boards you have not chosen to sync stay on your device. Sharing is opt-in per canvas: a private canvas is never registered with the server and its contents are never uploaded.

What we do not store

  • Card numbers. We never see them. Payment details go directly to our payment provider (below).
  • Your password, if you sign in with a provider that handles it.
  • Your location, your contacts, your browsing history, or your device identifiers.
  • Behavioural analytics. We do not record which widgets you use, what you type, how long you stay, where you click, or what your boards are called. The single usage event we do send is described below.

Usage counting

Grovepad counts how many times the app is opened, and nothing else. This is how we know whether anyone is using it — there is no other measurement anywhere in the product.

  • Exactly one event exists, called app_opened. The only detail we add to it is whether you opened Grovepad in a browser or as the installed app.
  • PostHog attaches what any website already sees: your browser and operating system, the address of the page, and a rough country worked out from your internet address. We have told PostHog to discard the address itself rather than store it.
  • A random number is kept on your device so that opening the app twice is not counted as two people. It is not linked to your account, your email, or anything you have made, and clearing your browser storage throws it away.
  • It never carries content. No board text, no widget contents, no filenames, no board or canvas names, no identifiers of things you made.
  • You can turn it off in Settings. It is on by default. When it is off, the analytics code is never even downloaded — there is no request to block, because no request is made.
  • We honour your browser's refusal. If you have Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control switched on, counting does not run regardless of the Settings toggle.

The counting is done by PostHog. If we ever add a second event, this list gets longer on the same day — the events are enumerated here by name precisely so that cannot happen quietly.

Optional things you turn on yourself

These are off until you switch them on, and each one only sends what it needs to:

  • On-device AI. The built-in local model runs entirely inside your browser. Your text is never sent anywhere.
  • Your own AI key. If you paste an API key from another provider, requests go from your device to that provider under their terms. We do not proxy or store them.
  • Canvas LMS. Your school access token is stored on your device and passed through our server only to reach your school's Canvas site. We do not keep the token or the coursework.
  • Calendar. If you connect a calendar, we read the events you asked for to show them on a board.

Getting your data out

Export is free, always, on every plan, and it will never be put behind a payment. You can export any board to JSON or Markdown from inside the app. Those are open formats you can read without Grovepad. We consider this a promise, not a feature.

How long we keep things

  • While your account is open: we keep your synced boards, attachments, and version history so they are there when you open the app.
  • If a paid plan lapses: your cloud copies are kept and remain downloadable for 90 days, with warnings inside the app, and are then deleted. Your local boards are untouched and stay fully editable.
  • If you delete your account: your account record, synced boards, attachments, version history and comments are deleted. Backups roll off within 30 days.
  • Billing records are kept by Polar for as long as tax law requires them to be kept.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it, or hand it to you in a portable format. In practice, export and account deletion inside the app do all three faster than we could by email — but write to us and we will do it by hand if you prefer. We reply within two working days.

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Grovepad is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. Guest mode requires no account at all. If you believe a child has an account with us, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Stored board documents and attachments are encrypted at rest by our hosting providers. Access to a shared canvas is enforced by the database itself, not just by the app, so removing someone removes their access to the pictures on that board in the same act. No system is perfect; if we ever have a breach that affects you, we will tell you directly and promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we change something that matters, we will say so in the app before it takes effect, not quietly edit this page. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
