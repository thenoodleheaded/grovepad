Refunds

You get 30 days free before any money moves, and a no-questions refund for 14 days after any charge. Nobody should pay for software they did not end up using.

Last updated: 12 August 2026

To ask for a refund: email support@grovepad.app from the address on your account. Say "refund". You do not have to explain why. We reply within two working days and the money goes back the way it came.

Before you are ever charged

Every new subscription starts with a 30-day free trial. A card is required to begin it, but nothing is taken until the trial ends. Cancel at any point in those 30 days and you are charged nothing at all — there is no refund to ask for, because there was no payment.

Grovepad also has a permanently free tier that is not a trial and never expires, so you can keep using the app after cancelling.

The 14-day refund

If you are charged and change your mind, ask within 14 days of that charge and we refund it in full. No questions, no forms, no "what went wrong" survey. This applies to:

  • the first payment after a trial converts;
  • a monthly payment;
  • a yearly renewal you did not mean to let through.

A refunded subscription ends immediately. Your local boards keep working exactly as before — that never changes — and your cloud copies stay downloadable for 90 days.

After 14 days

We do not automatically refund a charge older than 14 days, but we would still rather hear from you than have you feel stuck. If a renewal caught you by surprise, if the app broke for you, or if something we promised did not work, write to us and say so plainly. We handle these case by case and we lean towards saying yes.

Your legal rights

This policy is offered on top of your rights under consumer law, not instead of them. Nothing here removes a statutory right to cancel or to a refund that applies where you live. If you are in the EU or the UK, your right of withdrawal on digital purchases applies as the law provides.

How the money actually moves

Grovepad Air is sold by Polar as merchant of record, so Polar processes the refund and returns the money to your original payment method. Depending on your bank, it usually appears within 5 to 10 working days. Any tax or VAT charged at checkout is refunded with it.

You can also cancel a subscription yourself at any time from the billing portal linked in Grovepad's settings, which stops the next renewal without needing to email anyone.

Chargebacks

Please email us before opening a dispute with your bank. A chargeback costs us a flat fee on top of the refund and takes months, whereas an email is settled in a day or two. We have never refused a reasonable refund request and do not intend to start.
