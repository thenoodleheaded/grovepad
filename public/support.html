What to include

Any of these help, none are required:

  • What you were doing when it went wrong, in your own words.
  • Whether you were signed in or using guest mode.
  • Which device and browser, or which app (web, desktop, phone).
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.

Questions people ask most

Will I lose my boards if I stop paying?

No. Your boards live on your own device and stay fully editable forever, on any plan and on no plan. Syncing and other cloud features stop, and cloud copies remain downloadable for 90 days, but nothing local is ever locked or made read-only.

How do I get my work out of Grovepad?

Export any board to JSON or Markdown from inside the app. It is free on every plan, always, and we will never put it behind a payment. Those are open formats you can read without Grovepad.

Do I need an account?

No. Guest mode is a full, permanent mode — every widget, every skin, unlimited boards, all offline. An account only exists so your boards can sync between devices and so people can share boards with you.

Does someone need to pay to join a board I share?

Never. Joining is free for everyone, always. Only the person hosting the board needs a plan, and even then the free plan can host for 20 minutes per board per day.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings in Grovepad and follow the "manage subscription" link. It opens the billing portal, where you can cancel or change your card. You keep Air until the end of the period you already paid for. See refunds if you want money back too.

I am a student — how does the student price work?

Tick the student box on the pricing page and pay 19.99 USD per year, or 4.99 USD per month. We ask for no proof and no school email. It is on trust.

How do I delete my account and everything in it?

Ask us and we will do it, or delete it from Settings. Your account record, synced boards, attachments, version history and comments all go. Export first if you want to keep them — deletion is not reversible.

Is my board data private?

Boards are private by default. A canvas is never uploaded until you deliberately share it, and it is never public until you deliberately publish it. The one thing we measure is that the app was opened — never what is on a board — and you can switch even that off in Settings. The privacy page lists exactly what is stored.

Something is broken and I am losing work

Export the board immediately — that is always available and does not need the cloud — then email us with the file attached. Data loss goes to the front of the queue ahead of everything else.

Reporting a security problem

Email support@grovepad.app with "security" in the subject line and we will get back to you quickly. Please give us a reasonable window to fix it before publishing. We are grateful for the report and will credit you if you want.
