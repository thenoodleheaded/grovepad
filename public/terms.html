7. Fair-use limits on shared boards

Live shared boards cost us money in proportion to how much they are used, so they carry honest ceilings rather than an unbounded promise:

  • Attachments on Air: 10 GB, with 12 months of version history.

These are the only counted limits in Grovepad. We do not count local boards or local widgets, and we never will.
